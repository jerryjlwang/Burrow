## 7. Copy in the rabbit's own replies

Status: open. Requested 2026-09-20, from a scripted audit of 30 live replies on the demo pages.

- Em dashes in 9 of 30 replies (for example "Yep, this page is your Algebra I dashboard." and "Right here, the Sign in link." came back with an em dash where the comma is). The front end now replaces an em or en dash with a comma before it is shown (`extension/src/components/copy.ts`), but the spoken line still carries the pause, and the rule for this product is no em dashes anywhere. Please tell the model in `server/src/agent/prompt.ts`: no em dashes, no en dashes, short plain sentences.
- "What is on this page?" on the algebra practice page was answered with a question back ("What do you notice you'd do first to isolate x?"). A direct question about the page should get a direct answer; the Socratic turn belongs to hints, not to "what is this".
- "Where is the sign in button?" on the algebra page said "the Sign in link is in the footer area" while pointing correctly at the header link. The words should come from the element's actual position (the page model has the rect), or not name a position at all.
- Escape while a question is being answered cancels the answer (the universal interrupt in `extension/src/content/controller.ts`). Pressing Escape to close the panel is a natural thing to do right after asking; consider limiting the interrupt to speech and confirmations, and letting the reply land.
