# Character manifest contract

Every character is a folder under `extension/public/characters/<name>/` holding PNG strips and a `manifest.json`. The sprite player reads only the manifest. Nothing about a character is hard-coded in the player, so a new character is a new folder.

The rabbit's manifest is generated by `tools/sprites/rabbit_px.py`. Do not edit it by hand.

## Top level

- `character`: the folder name.
- `cell`: `[width, height]` of one frame in pixels. Strips are horizontal. Frame `i` starts at `x = i * width`.
- `anchor`: text. The player treats the bottom center of the cell as the feet.
- `body`: `[left, top, right, bottom]` pixel box of the character in idle frame 0, rim included, right and bottom exclusive. Use it for hit testing, dragging and placing speech bubbles. Everything outside it can be transparent margin.
- `jump_sequence.sending` and `jump_sequence.receiving`: ordered steps `{ "state", "reverse"?, "loop"? }`. `loop: true` means keep looping that state until the handoff confirms. A looping state as the final step names the resting pose; the player shows whatever state the app is asking for at that point instead of forcing it.
- `idle_variant_gap`: `[min, max]` seconds between random idle variants.
- `wander_gap`: `[min, max]` seconds of quiet idle before the character wanders a few hops on its own. `wander_hops`: `[min, max]` hops per wander. Wandering never happens while the panel is open, while speaking or listening, or under reduced motion.

## Per state

- `file`, `frames`, `fps`, `loop`, `notes`.
- `head_dy`: per frame vertical offset in pixels for overlays. Missing means 0 for every frame.
- `overlays`: which overlay strips may be drawn on top of this state, any of `"blink"`, `"mouth"`. Missing means none.
- `hold`: `true` means a non-looping state stays on its last frame until the state changes (`listening`). Missing or `false` means the player goes to `idle` after the last frame.
- `enter`: name of a non-looping state to play before this state starts.
- `exit`: name of a non-looping state to play when leaving this state, whatever the next state is.
- `variants`: idle only. `[{ "state", "weight" }]` one-shot flourishes the player picks at random between idle loops. Each ends on idle frame 0.
- `move`: per frame sideways travel in source pixels while this state plays as a travel loop (`hop`). The player multiplies by the scale and the direction, so he only moves on the frames that list a value.

## Overlay strips

- `overlay_blink`: 3 frames, half, closed, half. Play at its `fps` on a random timer, 2 to 5 seconds apart, only while the current state lists `"blink"`.
- `overlay_mouth`: 4 frames, 0 flat, 1 open, 2 wide, 3 smile. Only while the current state lists `"mouth"` and the character is speaking. Pick by loudness: below 0.15 is 0, below 0.5 is 1, otherwise 2. When not speaking draw nothing, the base art has its own mouth.
- Both overlays shift down by `head_dy[frame]` of the state underneath.

## Playback rules

- Draw at whole-number scales only, with image smoothing off and `image-rendering: pixelated`. Never let CSS produce a fractional scale.
- Change from state A to B: play `A.exit` if any, then `B.enter` if any, then B. Transition states are ordinary non-looping states. While a transition plays, further requests queue and the latest one applies when it ends.
- Every non-looping state ends on a frame that matches idle frame 0, or the first frame of the state it leads into, so cuts are clean.
- `reverse: true` plays a state from its last frame to its first.
- An unknown state name falls back to `idle` and logs once.

## State names today

Base states: `idle`, `listening`, `thinking`, `aha`, `confused`, `celebrate`, `wave`, `sleepy`, `dragged`, `hop`, `settle` (calm ending of a hop trip), `land` (startled landing after a throw), `panic` (hold, the "I'm late" beat before a hole trip), `hole_only`, `hole_open`, `dive`, `hole_wait`, `overlay_blink`, `overlay_mouth`.

Transitions, reached only through `enter` and `exit`: `to_listening`, `from_listening`, `to_thinking`, `to_confused`, `from_confused`, `to_sleepy`, `from_sleepy`. `thinking` exits through `aha`.

Idle variants, reached only through `idle.variants`: `idle_tap`, `idle_watch`, `idle_flick`. They list `blink` and `mouth` overlays so speech keeps working while one plays.

Other characters reuse these names. The player must not assume the list is fixed. The generator's `verify()` checks that every transition and variant ends on the frame it hands off to, and that no frame's rim is clipped by the cell edge.

## Characters made from a photo

`become.html` (popup: "Become a character") takes a picture with the laptop camera, or a photo file, and builds a whole character from it in the page with no server: `components/pet/pixelize.ts` cuts the background (flood fill from the edges within a colour tolerance of the border colour, then the largest blob only), snaps the colours to a median-cut palette, shrinks by majority vote so pupils and outlines survive, and adds the ink outline and cream rim. One base sprite then becomes every state above through whole-pixel moves (breath, squash, lean, lift, flip, a hole in rows 50 to 55, and a thought bubble, `?`, `!` and `z` glyphs), in the same 64 x 58 cell with the feet on row 53, so the player, hops, throws, wander and hole trips all work unchanged. There are no blink or mouth overlays, so speech is idle with the voice.

The result is stored once in `chrome.storage.local` under `burrow.customCharacter` (`id`, `label`, `manifest`, PNG data URL per state, about 80 KB) and chosen with the `character` setting as `custom:<id>`; `loadCharacter` reads it from there and falls back to the rabbit if it is missing. "Back to the rabbit" sets `character` to `rabbit`. Check: `python tools/pet/become/make-inputs.py` once, then `node extension/build.mjs && node tools/pet/become/check.mjs` (fake camera, 14 checks, screenshots in `tools/pet/become/shots`).

### Colouring in a black and white drawing

A drawing on paper has no colours to keep, so the tune step has a "Who is this?" line and a "Colour it in" button. `components/pet/regions.ts` splits the cut-out into the regions between its dark lines (4-connected, biggest first, at most 40), the page draws a number on each and posts that picture, the original photo and the kid's words to `POST /api/character/paint` (`server/src/api/character.ts`, one OpenAI vision call on `LLM_MODEL`, JSON in, `{name, colors: {id: "#rrggbb"}}` out; a stand-in palette without a key). The page fills each region with its colour, keeps the lines, and the usual pixelize runs on the coloured picture. "Undo colour" goes back to the photo as taken. The outline has to close around each part: a gap lets the background fill leak in and the part disappears with it.

### A pencil drawing is cut out by its lines

A photo of a drawing is not a patch of colour on a plain wall: the character is paper inside a faint outline, the paper is unevenly lit, and the outline has gaps, so the colour based background cut ate the whole drawing. `components/pet/sketch.ts` does it from first principles: estimate the paper brightness locally (a max filter wider than a stroke, then a blur), call anything darker than a fraction of that ink (so a desk edge or a shadow is not ink), thicken the ink a little so small gaps close, flood the outside in from the border through everything that is not ink, and keep the largest region of what the flood could not reach plus the ink itself. If the outline still leaks (little paper inside compared with the ink) the closing widens by itself. The result is a clean cut-out, ink in one dark colour and the inside white, that the pixel and colour-by-numbers steps treat like any picture. The page guesses drawing versus coloured picture from how grey the photo is and shows the guess as a toggle; the first slider becomes "Line darkness" for a drawing. Checked with `node tools/pet/become/check-pencil.mjs` (a made-up phone photo of a pencil Pikachu with a lit gradient, a desk edge and a gap in the outline: 8 of 8 against the live server).
