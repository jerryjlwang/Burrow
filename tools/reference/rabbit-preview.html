The White Rabbit, pixel by pixel

Every animation for the Wonderland OS hero, hand-placed on a 48 by 58 grid. Checked jacket, waistcoat, bow tie, and a pocket watch on a chain, all from the book. Pick a state, change the wallpaper, make him talk, then send him down the rabbit hole.

Wallpaper

Talking and blinking are small patches drawn over the idle loop. When he breathes, the head drops one pixel, and the manifest tells the renderer to move the patches with it.

    The jump between laptops

    He never walks off a screen. A hole opens under his feet, he drops in, and the hole closes. On the other laptop a hole opens, his ears poke out while the two machines shake hands, and then he pops up. A slow network just means the ears wait a little longer.

    Using the files

    Each state is one horizontal strip of 48 by 58 pixel cells, listed in manifest.json with its frame count, speed, and whether it loops. Scale by whole numbers and set image-rendering: pixelated. Every pixel is listed in rabbit_px.py as plain runs of color, so you can nudge a pixel there and regenerate, or open the strips in Aseprite or Piskel and edit by hand.
