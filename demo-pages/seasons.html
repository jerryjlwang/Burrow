Why do we have seasons?

Science 6 · Cross-curricular reading · Ms. Alvarez

Reading

Every year the days grow long and warm, then short and cold, in a cycle so reliable that whole calendars are built around it. Summer brings heat and late sunsets; winter brings frost and early dark. But what actually causes the seasons?

Here is a clue to start with: while it is summer in the United States, it is winter in Australia — at the very same moment, on the very same planet. Whatever explains the seasons has to explain that too.

Think about what you already believe causes summer, write it in your notes, and then test your idea against the clue above before reading the answer key in class.
