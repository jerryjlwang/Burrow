Solving two-step equations

Math 7 · Unit 3 · Video lesson · Mr. Chen

About this video

Mr. Chen solves 3x + 5 = 20 two ways, then shows the mistake almost everyone makes the first time: moving a number across the equals sign without changing what it does. Bring a pencil.
